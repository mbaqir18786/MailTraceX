⚡ How It Works: Sub-Second Email Blocking & Gov Human Review Pipeline

End-to-End Autonomous Email Defense with Sovereign Human-in-the-Loop Oversight.

Step 1
📩 Mail Received
When an email arrives at a citizen (e.g. M Baqir) or university inbox, MailTraceX gateway captures the raw envelope before rendering.
Step 2
⚡ < 1-Sec AI Check
Within 380ms, the AI engine evaluates SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication, domain age, malware hashes, and coercive NLP phrases.
Step 3
⛔ Auto-Blocked (>75)
If Risk Score > 75/100, the mail is immediately quarantined from the recipient's inbox and Section 65B forensic hash is sealed.
Step 4
🛡️ Gov Human Review
MeitY Nodal Officers inspect forensic reasons in gov.html and choose to [Unblock & Release] or [Maintain Block & Blacklist].
